Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects when you use our personal expense tracking application ("Service"), how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Data We Collect

We collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password when you register for an account. We also collect the expense data you enter — including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions — so that Spendly can display your spending history and summaries back to you.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to operate and improve the Service: authenticating your account, storing and displaying your expenses, generating category breakdowns and monthly summaries, and communicating important account or service updates. We do not use your financial data for advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely in our database and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time through the contact options available in the app, after which your data will be permanently removed within a reasonable timeframe.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell or share your personal or financial data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may rely on trusted third-party infrastructure providers (such as hosting or email delivery services) solely to operate the Service, and these providers are bound to handle your data only as instructed by us.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the contact options available within the app.

Your continued use of Spendly after any update to this Privacy Policy means you accept the updated terms.
